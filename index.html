Week 3 — Credit risk domain, CampusX se bahar

Day 14–20
14Sun

WOE aur Information Value

WOE = ln(% non-events ÷ % events) per bin. IV = poore variable ki predictive power. Rule of thumb: IV < 0.02 bekaar, 0.1–0.3 medium, > 0.5 shak karo (leakage ho sakta hai).

15Mon

Credit risk modelling — hands-on series

Data prep, target definition (kaun "bad" hai — 90+ DPD), aur sampling window. Yeh part tere project se missing hai kyunki germancredit mein target ready-made aata hai.

16Tue

Fine classing, coarse classing, monotonic binning

Tere paas min_perc_fine_bin=0.05 hai par woebin_adj nahi. Real scorecard mein bins manually adjust hote hain taaki bad rate monotonic rahe — ye sabse bada gap hai tere pipeline mein.

17Wed

Scorecard scaling — points0, odds0, PDO

Tere paas points0=600, odds0=1/19, pdo=50 hai. Matlab: 600 points pe odds 1:19, aur har 50 points pe odds double. Factor = PDO ÷ ln(2). Ye derive karna aana chahiye — points aasman se nahi girte.

18Thu

PSI aur model monitoring

Thresholds yaad karo: PSI < 0.1 stable, 0.1–0.25 watch, > 0.25 model rebuild. CSI bhi dekh lo — wo variable-level PSI hai, tere code mein nahi hai.

19Fri

Industry reference

Ye kitaab hi wo source hai jahan se saare blogs copy hote hain. Do cheezein tere project mein nahi hain aur interviewer poochhega: reject inference (jinko loan mila hi nahi unka kya) aur out-of-time validation (tera split random hai, time-based nahi).

20Sat

Likho aur defend karo

Limitations section zaroor daalna. 1000 rows ka dataset hai, PSI train-vs-test pe nikala hai (out-of-time nahi) — ye khud bata dena hamesha better hai.
